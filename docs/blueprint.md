# **App Name**: Visoma

## Core Features:

- AI-Driven Service & Solutions Presentation: Showcase Visoma's expertise in AI, Data, and Automation through detailed, interactive service descriptions and tailored industry solutions, visually aligning with the company's futuristic brand.
- Dynamic Portfolio & Case Studies Management: A system to store and display detailed client projects (Problem, Solution, Technology, Results), providing social proof and leveraging PostgreSQL for content persistence.
- Strategic Blog & Thought Leadership Hub: A dedicated section for publishing and managing articles on AI and data trends, reinforcing Visoma's position as an industry leader, with content stored in PostgreSQL.
- Direct Consultation Request Form: A clear, accessible contact form enabling potential clients to easily submit inquiries and book consultations.
- AI-Powered Content Refinement Tool (Internal): An internal AI tool leveraging Visoma's core capabilities to assist content creators in drafting and optimizing compelling summaries and taglines for service offerings and case studies.

## Style Guidelines:

- Background Palette: A deep, rich charcoal black (#0B0F0E) serving as the primary canvas, complemented by a slightly nuanced dark teal-gray secondary background (#102826), creating an immersive dark theme.
- Accent Palette: A sophisticated, muted gold (#C7A45A) as the brand's primary highlighting color, accentuated by a brighter, glowing gold (#FFD47A) to denote interactivity and futuristic elements, evoking premium trust and elegance.
- Typography Colors: Near-white (#E6E6E6) for primary text ensures high readability on the dark background, while a subtle teal-gray (#8B9B97) provides a softer secondary text color.
- Headline Font: 'Playfair Display' (serif), chosen for its elegant and modern aesthetic, conveying sophistication and high trust. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Body Font: 'Inter' (sans-serif), providing a clean, contemporary, and highly legible body text suitable for extended reading on a digital interface. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Icons: Sleek, geometric, and minimalist icons rendered in the accent gold, reinforcing the futuristic and premium visual identity, appearing within glassmorphism elements where applicable.
- Structure: A minimalist dark UI, utilizing generous negative space to emphasize key content. Features glassmorphism cards for depth and layered complexity, subtly enhanced by animated neural network or particle backgrounds, all within a fully responsive design.
- Transitions: Smooth, subtle animations and transitions via Framer Motion for scroll, hover effects, and component state changes, with an emphasis on soft glows to create an engaging and futuristic user experience.
